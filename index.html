Required 5-Step SWA Protocol (No Retaliation Policy):

  1. Stop Work Immediately: Cease all activities in the area and notify crew.
  2. Notify Supervisor or Lead: Report the unmitigated hazard immediately.
  3. Assess & Correct: Work with supervisor to bring in required PPE, shoring, LOTO, or gas monitor.
  4. Verify Safety Measures: Confirm hazard is 100% mitigated before touching equipment.
  5. Resume Work Only When Safe: Re-check the verification box below once corrected.
